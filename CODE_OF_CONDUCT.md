# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in Cloudflare Inbox a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology choices.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Keep technical criticism focused on the work, not the person.
- Protect private information and follow coordinated security disclosure.
- Accept moderation decisions and repair harm when asked.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository activity, project communication, events, and when representing the
project elsewhere. Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through the repository's
published contact channel. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers will
limit report access, consider conflicts of interest, and may remove content, issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation according to severity and
pattern.

Good-faith reports will not be retaliated against. Knowingly false reports or use of the process to
harass someone may themselves violate this policy.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
